The shift

School management shouldn't feel like managing paperwork.

Most schools didn't choose fragmentation — it accumulated. Mevo School is the step that puts it back together.

Stage 01Paper

Registers and files

Attendance in registers, fees in receipt books, results in folders. Nothing is searchable.

Stage 02Spreadsheets

Twelve versions of truth

Every department keeps its own sheet. Numbers stop matching by the second week of term.

Stage 03Multiple systems

Tools that don't talk

One tool for fees, another for messaging, another for results — and manual work between all of them.

Stage 04Mevo School

One connected record

Every student, class, payment and result in a single system that the whole school shares.
